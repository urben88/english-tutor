# Character spec: conversational assistant

## Purpose

A friendly English tutor that faces the learner at eye level, from the head to mid-chest, and reacts while the app listens, thinks and speaks.

## Visual description (from `assets/reference-images/expression-sheet.png`)

A young man with voluminous wavy brown hair swept over the forehead. He has black rectangular glasses, blue eyes, thick dark brows and a light-blue button-down shirt with a collar. The style is cartoon and semi-stylized: a slightly large head and soft shapes. `classroom-background.png` is a setting reference only and is used as the demo background.

**Likeness is stylized.** The model captures the recognizable traits, not a portrait of the person.

## Style and proportions

- Head ≈ 0.42 × shoulder width, short neck, rounded jaw.
- Primitive-based shapes (ellipsoids and swept tubes), smooth shaded, small procedural textures (skin grain, cheek blush, shirt weave, hair streaks).

## Scale, units, pivot

Metres, Y up (glTF), facing +Z. Origin at floor level under the character; head centre at y ≈ 1.62. The camera in `config/element.config.json` frames the bust.

## Meshes and materials

`Head` (with nose and ears), `Hair`, `Eyes` (white, iris, pupil, highlight), `Eyelids`, `Brows`, `Mouth` (interior + teeth), `Glasses`, `Body` (neck, shirt, collar, buttons, both arms and hands). Materials are glTF PBR (metalness 0), one per colour.

## Skeleton (21 bones, rigid skinning)

`hips › spine › chest › neck › head › eye_L/eye_R`, plus for each side `shoulder › upperarm › forearm › hand › index / fingers / thumb`. Gestures use the right arm (shown on the viewer's left). The left arm is rigged but rests out of frame.

## Expressions (combinations, in config)

`neutral`, `happy`, `listening`, `thinking`, `surprised`, `speaking`. Each one is a set of morph weights, plus an optional head offset and eye direction.

## Animations

All animations are procedural at runtime, not baked clips. Every layer eases toward its target, so any change blends and everything returns smoothly to idle.

| Kind | Items |
|---|---|
| Idle | breathing, head drift, eye saccades, random blinks (sometimes double) |
| Speech | `talk`: visemes + nod on emphasis + brow lift |
| Gestures | `wave`, `point`, `explain`, `listen`, `idea` |
| States | `idle`, `listening`, `thinking`, `speaking` |

The skill also lists `think`, `react` and `nod`. Here, `think` is the `thinking` expression (gaze up, head tilt), and `nod` is part of speech. A hand-to-chin `think` gesture is not included (see Known limitations in `VALIDATION-REPORT.md`).

## Interaction model

The host app drives it through the API: `setState`, `setExpression`, `gesture`, `speak`, `stopSpeaking`, `setSpeechLevel`. There is no pointer interaction.

## Accessibility

- The canvas has `role="img"` and an `aria-label` (from config). It conveys no information that is not already in the chat.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` scales idle motion and gesture oscillation down to 35–40 %.
- Failure is silent and leaves the rest of the UI usable.

## Performance

One WebGL canvas, three directional lights plus a hemisphere light, no shadows, pixel ratio capped at 2. The update loop allocates nothing per frame except small vectors at init.

## Export formats

- `assets/models/conversational-assistant.glb`: the runtime asset.
- `assets/models/conversational-assistant.blend`: Blender source, regenerated by `tools/build_model.py`.
- **`.vrm`: not exported.** The VRM 1.0 humanoid needs required leg bones that this bust lacks, plus meta and licence fields. It also needs `@pixiv/three-vrm` to load and validate. Shipping an unvalidated file would be worse than none. How to produce one later is in `INTEGRATION.md` → "Upgrading to VRM".
