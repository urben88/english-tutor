# Product — English Tutor

- **Audience:** one Spanish speaker practising spoken English, daily, on a PC or phone browser.
- **Primary action:** talk to the tutor (voice first); studying a word is one click away.
- **Flows:** talk -> hear a word -> Explain (Translation / Grammar / Examples) -> word lands in the study panel -> replay / re-explain / jump to the sentence -> resume an older session.
- **Screens:** one console screen with areas (Stage, Subtitles, Transcript drawer, Study panel).
- **Constraints:** UI in English, explanations in Spanish; no gamification; light + dark; reduced motion respected; no 3D library yet.
